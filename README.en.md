# FM Prism · for Football Manager 27

**FM Prism** reads your **Football Manager 27** save (Steam, Windows) and turns it, on [fmprism.com](https://fmprism.com), into:
- a dashboard for your club: lineup and rotation, training, matches, transfers and the season;
- a player search, with profiles with percentiles;
- a comparison tool;
- reports;
- images for social media.

Available in English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Turkish, French, German and Italian.

> Unofficial tool, not affiliated with Sports Interactive or SEGA. Football Manager is a trademark of its respective owners. FM Prism **only reads** your save: it never changes it.

## Install

The easy way is **FM Prism Companion**, a small Windows app:

1. Download it from [fmprism.com](https://fmprism.com/en) (“Download Companion” button), unzip it and open `FM Prism Companion.exe`.
2. With FM27 closed, it installs the plugin and BepInEx, the mod loader it needs, on its own, and keeps them up to date.
3. Open FM27. The first time it takes a few minutes longer to start.
4. In the game, **F9** takes a snapshot of your squad and **F10** lets you pick countries with all their leagues.
5. Open [fmprism.com](https://fmprism.com/en) with the Companion running: your snapshot appears on its own.

There are two other ways:
- **Without the Companion:** download `fm-prism-plugin.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/FM-Prism/fm-prism-plugin/releases/latest), unzip it and run `Instalar FM Prism.bat` (the installer) with FM27 closed.
- **With an AI that has access to your computer** (for example Claude Code or Codex). Tell it:
  > Install FM Prism on this computer following exactly https://github.com/FM-Prism/fm-prism-plugin/blob/main/INSTALL-WITH-AI.md and ask me for permission before every change.

There's a step-by-step illustrated guide inside the ZIP and on [fmprism.com](https://fmprism.com/descargas/fm-prism-guia-instalacion-en.pdf).

## Uninstall

From the Companion's Home screen with “Uninstall…”, or with `Desinstalar FM Prism.bat` from the ZIP. Your snapshots are never deleted.

## Problems

Open an *issue* with a screenshot and the `BepInEx\LogOutput.log` file from your FM27 folder.
